# Aleksandr Mitroshenkov

Creator and developer of **Voxprint AI Media Suite**, a family of free, open-source desktop apps that run modern speech AI locally on your own PC, with no cloud and no subscriptions.

- 🎙️ [Voxprint AI Audiobook Builder](https://github.com/Mitroshenkov87/voxprint-audiobook-builder) turns books into audiobooks in your own trained voice, re-voices recordings and supports five UI languages.
- 🎬 Voxprint AI Movie Dubber adds a dubbed voice track to films (in development).
- 🖼️ Voxprint AI image and video generator: local, uncensored creative freedom (planned).

## What I stand for

- Freedom of speech and access to information, without censorship.
- Your data stays on your device: private, offline, under your control.
- Open source and open licences, so anyone can use, study and improve the tools.
